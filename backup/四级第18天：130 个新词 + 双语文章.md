## 双语文章 · Riding Shared Bikes Through the City / 骑着共享单车穿行城市

> 读法: 先读英文段落,遇到**加粗**的今日新词停一下回想意思,再看下面的中文。

---

Every morning I ride a shared bike to work. My flat is in the **northeast** of the city, and my office is near the old **stadium**, about five kilometers away.
