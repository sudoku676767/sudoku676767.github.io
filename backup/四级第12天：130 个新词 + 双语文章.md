## 双语文章 · A Night Under the Stars / 星空下的一夜

> 读法: 先读英文段落,遇到**加粗**的今日新词停一下回想意思,再看下面的中文。

---

Last Friday, our university astronomy club organized a trip to a **remote** **valley** two hours from the city.
