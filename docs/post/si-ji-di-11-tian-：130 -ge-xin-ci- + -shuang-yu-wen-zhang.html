双语文章 · Is Salary the Most Important Thing in a Job? / 工资是工作中最重要的吗?

读法: 先读英文段落,遇到加粗的今日新词停一下回想意思,再看下面的中文。

When junior students start looking for jobs, the first question they often ask is: "What is the salary?" It is a reasonable question. After graduation, young people need money for rent, food and maybe a loan. But many career experts advise students not to make salary their only consideration. "A high wage is nice," one specialist said, "but it is not the ultimate measure of a good job."

当低年级学生开始找工作时,他们最常问的第一个问题是:"工资是多少?"这是个合理的问题。毕业后,年轻人需要钱付房租、买食物,也许还要还贷款。但许多职业专家建议学生不要把工资当作唯一的考虑因素。"高薪水固然好,"一位专家说,"但它不是衡量好工作的终极标准。"

Take the story of Chen Yu. After graduating, she accepted an offer from a big corporation with a substantial salary. At first she was excited.
