双语文章 · How Small Habits Change Your Life / 小习惯如何改变你的生活

读法: 先读英文段落,遇到加粗的今日新词停一下回想意思,再看下面的中文。

Many students believe that success comes from big moments: a perfect exam, a lucky chance, a sudden passion. But psychologists say the truth is more ordinary. What really shapes our lives are small daily habits. "Habits are the fundamental building blocks of who we become," said one psychological researcher. "They dominate about forty percent of what we do each day."

很多学生认为成功来自重大时刻:一次完美的考试、一个幸运的机会、一股突然的热情。但心理学家说,真相要平凡得多。真正塑造我们生活的是日常的小习惯。"习惯是我们成为什么样的人的基本构件,"一位心理学研究者说。"它们支配着我们每天大约百分之四十的行为。"

Take the example of Wang Hao, a teenager who used to stay up until 2 a.m. playing games. He was always tired, often nervous before tests, and his grades were falling. One day, he read an article about habits and decided to adjust his routine. He did not try to change everything at once. Instead, he made one small promise: go to bed thirty minutes earlier each week.

以王浩为例,这个青少年过去常常玩游戏到凌晨两点。他总是很累,考试前经常紧张,成绩不断下滑。有一天,他读了一篇关于习惯的文章,决定调整自己的作息。他没有试图一下子改变一切,而是给自己定了一个小承诺:每周提早三十分钟睡觉。

The result was surprising. After two months, he was sleeping eight hours a night. He could concentrate better in class, and his mood improved. "I used to feel angry for no reason," he said. "Now the anger is gone, and I feel more capable of handling stress." He then added another habit: reading for twenty minutes before bed.
