双语文章 · Saving the Forest / 拯救森林

读法: 先读英文段落,遇到加粗的今日新词停一下回想意思,再看下面的中文。

At the beginning of the year, a small village in the southern hills faced a crisis. A company wanted to cut down the old forest behind the village and build a factory. The plan would bring jobs and income, but many citizens saw it as a threat to their way of life. The forest was home to a variety of animal species, and for local people it was part of their tradition and spirit.

年初,南部山区的一个小村庄面临一场危机。一家公司想砍掉村子后面的古老森林,建一座工厂。这个计划会带来工作和收入,但许多村民(citizen 公民)把它看作是对他们生活方式的威胁。这片森林是多种动物物种的家园,对当地人来说,它也是他们传统和精神的一部分。

The debate was immediately heated. Some people assumed the factory was the only option for a poor village. Others argued that once the trees were gone, they could never be replaced. A senior teacher named Mr. Chen refused to stay silent. "We have a responsibility to our children," he said in a speech at the village hall. "We should not suffer later for a quick profit now."

这场辩论****立刻激烈起来。有些人认为(assume 假定)工厂是这个贫困村庄唯一的选择。另一些人则主张,树一旦没了就再也无法替代。一位名叫陈先生的资深教师拒绝沉默。"我们对孩子们负有责任,"他在村礼堂的一次演讲中说。"我们不应该为了眼前的快钱,让以后受苦。"

Mr. Chen and a dozen volunteers decided to conduct their own investigation. With help from a scientist at a nearby university, they did a survey of the forest.
