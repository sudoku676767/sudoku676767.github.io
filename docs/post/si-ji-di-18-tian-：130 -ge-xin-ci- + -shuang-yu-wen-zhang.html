双语文章 · Riding Shared Bikes Through the City / 骑着共享单车穿行城市

读法: 先读英文段落,遇到加粗的今日新词停一下回想意思,再看下面的中文。

Every morning I ride a shared bike to work. My flat is in the northeast of the city, and my office is near the old stadium, about five kilometers away. Before bike sharing came, I took a bus that was always crowded and slow. I had to tolerate people pushing me and a driver who used the horn like a drum. Now I simply scan a code, and the lock opens instantly.

每天早上我都骑共享单车上班。我的公寓在城市的东北部,办公室在老体育场附近,大约五公里远。共享单车出现之前,我坐的公交车总是又挤又慢。我得忍受别人推挤,还有一位把喇叭按得像敲鼓一样的司机。现在我只要扫一下二维码,车锁就立刻打开了。

The bikes are light because their frames are made of aluminum. Each one has a basket, a rack at the back and a flexible seat that you can raise or lower to fit your height. Some companies even equip their bikes with GPS so that they can be found quickly and parked safely. A friend of mine who works as a mechanic told me that the reliability of these bikes has improved a lot in recent years.

这些单车很轻,因为车架是铝做的。每辆车都有一个车筐、一个后架和一个可灵活调节的座椅,你可以根据身高把它调高或调低。有些公司甚至给单车配备了GPS,这样车就能被快速找到并安全停放。我有个当机修工的朋友告诉我,这些单车的可靠性近几年提高了很多。

Riding is good for my health, too. When I arrive, my pulse is fast and my legs feel a bit stiff, but my mind is clear. In spring, a gentle breeze blows along the river lane, and I sometimes see a bee flying among the flowers. I always wear a helmet, because my mother keeps saying, "Whoever rides without one is asking for trouble." Fortunately, I have never had an accident.

骑车对我的健康也有好处。到达时,我的脉搏跳得很快,双腿有点僵硬,但头脑很清醒。春天,河边的小路上吹着轻柔的微风,我有时会看到一只蜜蜂在花丛中飞舞。我总是戴着头盔,因为妈妈一直说:"无论谁不戴头盔骑车,都是在自找麻烦。"幸运的是,我从没出过事故。

Of course, the system is not perfect. Some people park bikes in the midst of the sidewalk and block the way. Others leave them in shallow water by the river or throw them into bushes out of spite. Newspapers condemn such behavior, and the city has passed a new statute that fines riders who park in the wrong places. In some corners, piles of broken bikes look like the ruins of a lost empire.

当然,这个系统并不完美。有些人把单车停在人行道中间,挡住了路。还有人把车丢在河边的浅水里,或者出于恶意把车扔进灌木丛。报纸谴责这种行为,市里也通过了一项新法规,对乱停车的骑行者进行罚款。在一些角落里,成堆的破单车看起来就像一个消失的帝国留下的废墟。

The government sees bike sharing as a progressive idea. The Ministry of Transport gives a subsidy to companies that keep their bikes in good order.
