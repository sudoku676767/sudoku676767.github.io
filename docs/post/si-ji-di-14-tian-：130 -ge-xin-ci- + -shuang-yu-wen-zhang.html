双语文章 · The Science Workshop / 科学工作坊

读法: 先读英文段落,遇到加粗的今日新词停一下回想意思,再看下面的中文。

I used to think science was boring. In high school, I was not curious about chemistry at all, and I did badly in every examination. So when my roommate asked me to join a weekend science workshop, I almost said no. "It's not like class," she promised. "It's fun. You will be impressed." I was not eager, but I went anyway.

我以前觉得科学很无聊。高中时我对化学一点也不好奇,每次考试都考得很差。所以当室友邀请我参加周末的科学工作坊时,我差点拒绝。"这和上课不一样,"她保证说。"很好玩,你会印象深刻的。"我并不热衷,但还是去了。

The workshop was held in a big laboratory on campus. Our teacher was a young researcher with a great sense of humor. "Today we will not memorize anything," she said. "We will explode things—safely." Everyone laughed. She gave each group a simple assignment: make a small rocket using acid, powder and a plastic bottle.

工作坊在校园里的一个大实验室举行。我们的老师是一位很有幽默感的年轻研究员。"今天我们不背任何东西,"她说。"我们要让东西爆炸,当然是安全地。"大家都笑了。她给每个小组布置了一个简单的任务:用酸、粉末和一个塑料瓶做一个小火箭。

At first, our group made every mistake possible. We used the wrong proportion of materials, and our rocket only made a sad little sound. But the teacher did not give up on us. She explained the ratio clearly and showed us how the gas pushes the bottle up. On our third try, the rocket shot high into the sky with a loud noise. We cheered like children.
