双语文章 · Can Online Learning Replace the Classroom? / 网课能取代课堂吗?

读法: 先读英文段落,遇到加粗的今日新词停一下回想意思,再看下面的中文。

Over the past few years, online learning has expanded quickly. With a smartphone or another device, students can now search for courses on almost any subject. They can explore literature, study scientific ideas, or learn a musical instrument without leaving home. This raises an important question: can the internet replace the traditional classroom?

过去几年里,网络学习迅速扩展。借助智能手机或其他设备,学生现在几乎可以搜索任何学科的课程。他们可以探索****文学,学习科学概念,或者学一种乐器,都不用出家门。这引出了一个重要问题:互联网能取代传统课堂吗?

Supporters say online learning has many advantages. It is relatively cheap, and students can study at their own speed. A farmer in a remote county can take the same course as a student in a big urban university. People who work all day can watch daily lessons at night. "It gives everyone a fair chance to obtain knowledge," said one academic who organizes free online courses. "Where you were born should not decide what you can learn."

支持者说网课有很多好处。它相对便宜,学生可以按自己的节奏学习。偏远县里的农民可以和大城市大学的学生上同样的课。整天工作的人可以在晚上看每日课程。"它给了每个人获得知识的公平机会,"一位组织免费网课的学者说。"你在哪里出生,不应该决定你能学到什么。"

However, critics remind us that learning is not just about information. A primary school teacher I spoke to said, "Children need to participate, ask questions and see how others react. A screen cannot read a child's expression or notice when she is sad." Some studies have found that students who study only online are more likely to feel lonely, and this can have negative effects on their mental and emotional health.

然而,批评者提醒我们,学习不仅仅是获取信息。我采访的一位小学老师说:"孩子们需要参与、提问,看别人如何反应。屏幕读不懂孩子的表情,也注意不到她什么时候难过。"一些研究发现,只在网上学习的学生更容易感到孤独,这可能对他们的心理和情绪健康产生负面影响。

There is also the problem of focus. At home, it is easy to shut the lesson window and start watching videos. "Online courses depend on self-control," said a university colleague of mine. "Without a teacher in the room, many students disappear after the first few weeks." In fact, some findings show that fewer than ten percent of people who sign up for free online courses complete them.

还有专注的问题。在家里,很容易关掉课程窗口,开始看视频。"网课依赖自制力,"我在大学的一位同事说。"没有老师在教室里,很多学生在头几周后就消失了。"事实上,一些研究发现表明,报名免费网课的人中,完成课程的不到百分之十。

So what is the best solution? Many experts now predict that the future will combine both. This is called "blended learning." Students watch short videos at home, and then come to class to discuss, repeat experiments and work in groups. Teachers become guides, not just speakers.
