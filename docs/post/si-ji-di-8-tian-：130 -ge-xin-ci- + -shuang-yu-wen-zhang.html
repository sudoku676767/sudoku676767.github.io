双语文章 · Going Home to Build the Future / 回乡建设未来

读法: 先读英文段落,遇到加粗的今日新词停一下回想意思,再看下面的中文。

After graduating, most of Liu Fang's classmates chose to work in big cities. Liu, a young engineer, made a rare choice: she went back to her rural hometown. "Many people thought I was crazy," she said. "They said I could earn much more in the city. But I wanted to use my talent where it was really needed."

毕业后,刘芳的大多数同学都选择在大城市工作。刘芳是一名年轻的工程师,她做了一个少见的选择:回到农村老家。"很多人觉得我疯了,"她说。"他们说我在城里能挣得多得多。但我想把我的才能用在真正需要的地方。"

Her village had a terrible problem with water. In summer, the old pipes often broke, and families had to walk a long route to a river to get water. Liu's initial plan was simple: fix the pipes. But after talking with local people, she realized that the problem was more vast. The village needed a solid new system, but no one could afford it.

她的村子有一个严重(terrible 可怕的)的用水问题。夏天旧水管经常破裂,家家户户只好走很远的路去河边取水。刘芳最初的计划很简单:修好水管。但在和村民交谈后,她意识到问题大得多(vast 巨大的)。村子需要一套牢固的新系统,但没人负担得起。

So Liu wrote an appeal to the local government and several companies. She emphasized that clean water was an essential need, not a luxury.
