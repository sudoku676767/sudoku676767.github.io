双语文章 · Spring Festival at My Grandparents' Home / 在爷爷奶奶家过春节

读法: 先读英文段落,遇到加粗的今日新词停一下回想意思,再看下面的中文。

Every year before the Spring Festival, my family travels back to my grandparents' village. The trains are packed, because hundreds of millions of people are doing the same thing. It is often called the largest transport event worldwide. This year I had a slight fever the day before we left, but it had partially gone by the time we arrived. Grandma said the fresh country air would heal me. I was so tired that I slept most of the way; consequently, I felt much better when we got off the train.

每年春节前,我们一家都会回爷爷奶奶住的村子。火车上挤满了人,因为数以亿计的人都在做同样的事情。这常被称为全世界规模最大的运输活动。今年出发前一天我有点发烧,但到达时已经部分退了。奶奶说乡下的新鲜空气会让我痊愈。我太累了,一路上大部分时间都在睡觉;所以下火车时我感觉好多了。

My grandparents' house comprises two floors and a small yard. The moment we stepped in, I smelled the scent of meat and wheat flour coming from the kitchen. Grandma was standing by the stove, and a big stack of dumpling wrappers lay on a tray beside her. Grandpa was upstairs, looking for the red paper decorations that he kept in an old sack.

爷爷奶奶的房子包括两层楼和一个小院子。我们一进门,就闻到厨房里飘来肉和小麦面粉的香味。奶奶站在炉子旁,她身边的托盘上放着一大叠饺子皮。爷爷在楼上,寻找他放在一个旧麻袋里的红纸装饰。

On New Year's Eve, the whole family worked together. My uncle climbed a ladder to hang red lanterns, while my little cousin, who is usually shy, helped me put the couplets on the door. Grandma rolled up her sleeves and taught us how to make dumplings. She showed us how to dip a finger in water, wet the edges, fold them and pat them gently. She hid a coin inside one dumpling and said that whoever found it would have good luck all year. "No peeking! Don't cheat!" she laughed.

除夕那天,全家人一起忙活。叔叔爬上梯子挂红灯笼,平时很害羞的小表弟帮我把春联贴到门上。奶奶挽起袖子,教我们包饺子。她示范怎样把手指蘸一下水,润湿边缘,再对折,然后轻轻拍一拍。她在一个饺子里藏了一枚硬币,说谁吃到它,一整年都会有好运。"不许偷看!不许作弊!"她笑着说。

Dinner was a real triumph. The table was covered with fish, pork ribs, chicken and carrots cut into the shape of flowers. When Grandma lifted the lid of the big pot, steam rose to the ceiling. Grandpa poured a sweet liquid made from rice into small cups and proclaimed that no one could leave the table before trying every dish. We chewed, talked and laughed for hours. Even my aunt, who strictly eats only healthy food, gave in to her hunger that night.
