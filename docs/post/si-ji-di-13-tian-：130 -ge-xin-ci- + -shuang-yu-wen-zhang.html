双语文章 · My Grandfather's Wisdom / 爷爷的智慧

读法: 先读英文段落,遇到加粗的今日新词停一下回想意思,再看下面的中文。

My grandfather is eighty-two years old, but he is still steady on his feet and full of energy. He lives in a small house in the countryside with a green lawn in front and a porch where he sits every evening. When I was a child, I thought he was just an ordinary elderly man. Gradually, I realized that he was one of the wisest people I knew.

我的爷爷八十二岁了,但他走路依然稳健,精力充沛。他住在乡下的一座小房子里,屋前有一片绿色的草坪,还有一个他每晚都坐着的门廊。小时候,我以为他只是一个普通的老人。渐渐地,我意识到他是我认识的最有智慧的人之一。

Grandfather was a teacher for forty years. His profession taught him patience. "Every child has a different imagination," he once told me. "A good teacher doesn't squeeze all students into one shape. You have to adapt to each one." Many of his former students still visit him. Some became doctors, some became engineers, and one even became a famous poet.

爷爷当了四十年老师。他的职业教会了他耐心。"每个孩子的想象力都不一样,"他曾经告诉我。"好老师不会把所有学生都硬塞进一个模子里。你必须适应每一个孩子。"他的很多学生至今还来看望他。有的成了医生,有的成了工程师,还有一个甚至成了著名诗人。

What I admire most is his attitude toward change. When the village got the internet, many old people refused to use it. But grandfather was curious. He asked me to teach him how to send messages and watch videos. "The world keeps changing," he said. "If you don't learn, you will fade away." Now he video-calls me every week and even reads poetry online.

我最钦佩的是他对待变化的态度。村里通网的时候,很多老人拒绝使用。但爷爷很好奇。他让我教他发消息、看视频。"世界一直在变,"他说。"如果你不学习,就会慢慢被淘汰(fade 褪色)。"现在他每周都和我视频通话,甚至还在网上读诗。

Still, grandfather believes some old customs should be kept. Every spring festival, he makes soup with honey and herbs, a recipe passed down through his tribe—as he jokingly calls our big family. "Traditions are like a framework," he explains. "They give our lives stability. Without them, we feel lost." He worries that young people consume too much and forget the value of simple things.
