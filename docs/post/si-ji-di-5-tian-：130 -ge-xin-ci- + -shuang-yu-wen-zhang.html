双语文章 · My First Semester in a Campus Club / 我在校园社团的第一个学期

读法: 先读英文段落,遇到加粗的今日新词停一下回想意思,再看下面的中文。

When I arrived at university, I was shy and hardly spoke to anyone. My roommate, a native of a small folk village in Yunnan, was the opposite. She was active, friendly and willing to try anything. In the first week, she said, "Let's join a club. It's the best way to gain friends and earn some experience." I almost refused, but in the end I agreed.

刚到大学时,我很害羞,几乎不和任何人说话。我的室友是云南一个小民间村落的本地人,她恰恰相反。她活泼、友善,愿意尝试任何事。第一周她就说:"我们加入一个社团吧,这是获得朋友、积累(earn 赚得)经验的最好办法。"我差点拒绝,但最后还是同意了。

There were dozens of clubs at the club fair, and a big crowd of students moved from table to table. We stopped at the table of the campus newspaper. The editor, a third-year student, explained that every reporter had to deliver one story each week. "It's hard work," she said, "but you will observe how the university really works, and you will learn useful techniques for writing." I was slightly nervous, but I signed my name.
