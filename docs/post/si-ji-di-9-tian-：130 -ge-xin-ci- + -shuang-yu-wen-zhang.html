双语文章 · A Student Lab Fights Climate Change / 一个学生实验室对抗气候变化

读法: 先读英文段落,遇到加粗的今日新词停一下回想意思,再看下面的中文。

On our campus, there is a small lab in the corner of the science building. From the outside it looks ordinary, but inside, a group of students is working on one of the biggest problems of our time: climate change. Their leader is Zhou Min, a graduate student who studies environmental science.
