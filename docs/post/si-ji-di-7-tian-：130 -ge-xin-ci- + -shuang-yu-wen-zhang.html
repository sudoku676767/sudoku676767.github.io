双语文章 · A Weekend in an Ancient Town / 古镇周末游

读法: 先读英文段落,遇到加粗的今日新词停一下回想意思,再看下面的中文。

Last weekend, my friends and I visited an ancient town by the ocean. Our teacher had recommended it, saying it was one of the most unique places in the province. We bought cheap train tickets, packed light, and somehow managed to get up at five in the morning. It turned out to be a trip full of beauty, surprises and one small accident.

上周末,我和朋友们去了海边的一座古老小镇。老师推荐了这个地方,说它是全省最独特的地方之一。我们买了便宜的火车票,轻装出发,不知怎么居然在早上五点起了床。结果这是一次充满美景、惊喜和一个小意外的旅行。

The town is located at the end of a long mountain trail. When we arrived, a local guide met us at the old stone gate. He told us that the town was more than eight hundred years old. "Look at the shadow of the gate on the ground," he said. "In the past, people used it to tell the time." He spoke with such silence between his sentences that every word felt important.

小镇位于一条长长的山间小路尽头。我们到达时,一位当地导游在古老的石门前迎接我们。他告诉我们这个小镇已有八百多年的历史。"看地上石门的影子,"他说。"过去人们用它来看时间。"他说话时句与句之间留着沉默,让每个字都显得很重要。

Our first stop was the town museum. It held old maps, golden coins and a novel written by a local writer two hundred years ago. The theme of the book was the struggle of fishermen against the sea. I truly appreciated the way the museum told the town's story—not with long texts, but with objects, photos and simple notes. The visitor book was full of comments in many languages, including German.
