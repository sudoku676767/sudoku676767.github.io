双语文章 · Shopping with a Click / 指尖上的购物

读法: 先读英文段落,遇到加粗的今日新词停一下回想意思,再看下面的中文。

I am an undergraduate, and like most youngsters today, I do most of my shopping on my mobile phone. The convenience is hard to beat. I can order a sweater, a new light bulb or a box of delicious grapes in a few seconds, and the package often arrives overnight. My grandmother, who seldom leaves her small cottage in the countryside, finds this amazing.

我是一名大学本科生,和如今大多数年轻人一样,我的大部分东西都是用手机买的。这种便利难以替代。我几秒钟就能订一件毛衣、一个新灯泡或一盒美味的葡萄,包裹往往第二天就到了。我奶奶很少离开她在乡下的小屋,她觉得这太神奇了。

Online shopping has steadily changed people's habits over the past ten years. Shopping festivals now prevail over some traditional holidays, and many people mark them on their calendars months ahead. During one big sale last year, I set an alarm and waited for midnight like a brave hunter. When the clock struck twelve, I clicked as fast as I could, hoping to get a bargain. Every seller offered coupons, and the whole process felt like a game.

过去十年里,网购稳步改变着人们的习惯。如今购物节的风头已经胜过了一些传统节日,许多人提前几个月就在日历上把它们标出来。去年一次大促时,我定了闹钟,像一个勇敢的猎人一样等待午夜。钟声一响,我就以最快的速度点击,希望抢到一件便宜货。每个卖家都在发优惠券,整个过程就像一场游戏。

Behind every click there is a huge system. Cargo ships bring goods from overseas and unload them at the dock, and then trucks carry them across the country. In large warehouses, workers and skilled technicians coordinate with each other so that orders can be packed quickly. Some companies even use drones to accelerate delivery. In the meantime, information about each package is transmitted to the buyer's phone, so you always know where it is.

每一次点击背后都有一个庞大的系统。货轮把商品从海外运来,在码头卸货,然后卡车把它们运往全国各地。在大型仓库里,工人和熟练的技术人员相互协调,让订单能被快速打包。有些公司甚至用无人机来加快配送。与此同时,每个包裹的信息都会传送到买家的手机上,所以你随时都知道它到了哪里。

However, online shopping is not always a delight. Once I bought a pair of shoes because the photos looked great, but when they arrived, the rubber soles smelled bad and the thread was coming loose. The description had been vague, and the size chart did not specify whether the sizes were Chinese or European. I felt very unhappy. When I asked for my money back, the shop sent a long apology but said that the coupon I had used was no longer valid. Dealing with it gave me a real headache.
