双语文章 · A Charity Art Exhibition / 一场慈善画展

读法: 先读英文段落,遇到加粗的今日新词停一下回想意思,再看下面的中文。

Last spring, our university art club decided to hold a charity exhibition to raise money for children in a mountain village. The idea came from our captain, Lin, a quiet painter who had visited the village during the winter break. "The school there has no heating, and the children study in old jeans and thin coats," she told us. "We can't solve every problem, but we can do something." It was a small venture, but nobody wanted to say no.

去年春天,我们大学的美术社决定举办一场慈善画展,为一个山村的孩子们筹款。这个主意来自我们的社长林,一位安静的画家,她寒假时去过那个村子。"那里的学校没有暖气,孩子们穿着旧牛仔裤和薄外套上课,"她告诉我们。"我们不可能解决所有问题,但可以做点什么。"这是一次小小的冒险尝试,但没人想说不。

At first, there was a lot of confusion. We had no sponsor, no gallery, and no clue how to sell paintings. Our first meeting was a mess: people kept interrupting each other, and one boy left upset. Then Lin made a simple remark: "Let's stop arguing and make a concrete plan." She divided us into teams. One team would recruit artists, one would find a place, and one would deal with money.

一开始,大家一片混乱。我们没有赞助商,没有画廊,也完全没有头绪怎么卖画。第一次开会乱成一团:大家不停地打断彼此,一个男生生气地走了。后来林说了一句简单的话:"别吵了,我们来做一个具体的计划。"她把我们分成几个小组:一组招募艺术家,一组找场地,一组管钱。
