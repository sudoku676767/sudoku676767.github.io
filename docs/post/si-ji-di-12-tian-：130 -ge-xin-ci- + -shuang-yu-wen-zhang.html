双语文章 · A Night Under the Stars / 星空下的一夜

读法: 先读英文段落,遇到加粗的今日新词停一下回想意思,再看下面的中文。

Last Friday, our university astronomy club organized a trip to a remote valley two hours from the city. There were roughly thirty of us, a whole bunch of students with bags, blankets and one big telescope. Our leader, a physics student named Gao, told us, "Out there, far from city lights, you will see the sky as people saw it thousands of years ago."

上周五,我们大学的天文社组织了一次旅行,去了离城市两小时车程的一个偏远****山谷。我们大约有三十人,一大群学生带着背包、毯子和一台大望远镜。领队是物理系学生高同学,他告诉我们:"在那里,远离城市灯光,你会看到几千年前人们看到的星空。"

We arrived just before sunset. While we set up the equipment, Gao explained how the telescope works. "It collects light and makes distant objects look closer," he said. "But you have to be patient. Your eyes need about twenty minutes to adjust to the dark." Some of us were surprised that he asked us to turn off our phones. "Even a small screen can ruin your night vision," he said.

我们在日落前到达。在安装设备时,高同学解释了望远镜的工作原理。"它收集光线,让远处的物体看起来更近,"他说。"但你得有耐心。眼睛大约需要二十分钟来适应黑暗。"有些人很惊讶他要我们关掉手机。"即使是一块小屏幕也会破坏你的夜视能力,"他说。

When the sky became completely dark, the view was remarkable. Thousands of stars filled the sky, and we could clearly see the Milky Way. Through the telescope, we saw the rings of Saturn and four of Jupiter's moons. One girl, who had never seen the stars like this, said it was the greatest joy of her lifetime. "It makes me feel so small," she whispered, "but also part of something tremendous."

当天完全黑下来时,景色非常壮观(remarkable 非凡的)。成千上万颗星星布满天空,我们能清楚地看到银河。透过望远镜,我们看到了土星的光环和木星的四颗卫星。一个从未这样看过星星的女孩说,这是她一生中最大的快乐。"它让我觉得自己很渺小,"她低声说,"但也是某种无比巨大的东西的一部分。"

Gao then told us about the history of astronomy. Long ago, people used the stars to interpret the seasons, guide ships to port, and even predict their fortune. Later, scientists began to detect patterns and evolve new theories. "The scientific revolution changed everything," he said. "People stopped believing the Earth was the center of the universe." Today, advanced technology lets us send aircraft and satellites far into space and study solar activity in detail.

高同学接着给我们讲了天文学的历史。很久以前,人们用星星来解读季节、引导船只进港,甚至预测自己的命运。后来,科学家开始发现规律,发展出新的理论。"科学革命改变了一切,"他说。"人们不再相信地球是宇宙的中心。"如今,先进的技术让我们能把飞行器和卫星送入太空深处,并详细研究太阳活动。

Around midnight, the temperature dropped and some students began to freeze. We made hot tea and sat in a circle. Someone asked a big question: "Is there life on other planets?"
