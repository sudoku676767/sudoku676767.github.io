双语文章 · An Unforgettable Field Trip / 一次难忘的野外考察

读法: 先读英文段落,遇到加粗的今日新词停一下回想意思,再看下面的中文。

Every autumn, our geography department organizes an outdoor field trip. This year, after a long lecture on rocks and rivers, our professor announced that we would visit a famous limestone cave in the southwest of the province. "You will see crystal formations that took millions of years to grow," he said. His enthusiasm motivated even the laziest students. Thirty of us packed sandwiches, towels and raincoats and set off by bus on a pleasant Saturday morning.

每年秋天,我们地理系都会组织一次户外考察。今年,在一场关于岩石和河流的长讲座之后,教授宣布我们将去参观本省西南部一个著名的石灰岩洞穴。"你们会看到花了几百万年才长成的水晶状岩层,"他说。他的热情甚至激励了最懒的学生。一个宜人的周六早上,我们三十个人带着三明治、毛巾和雨衣,坐大巴出发了。

The cave was near an old temple and a quiet pond where ducks swam and cattle drank. Before we entered, our guide gave us strict rules.
